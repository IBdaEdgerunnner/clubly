STUDENT AFFAIRS & CLUB GUIDELINES

Can't Find the Club You Want?

Here is why a specific organization might not be listed on CampusConnect yet, and how you can officially pitch or start your own community.

Why isn't every campus club listed?

CampusConnect currently features official recognized student organizations, faculty societies, and active hobbyist guilds that have completed registration with the university student affairs office. Newly forming or informal study groups are added to the directory periodically once faculty sponsorship and leadership are verified.

How to propose a new club

If your passion or interest group isn't represented yet, you have the power to start it! To establish a new club on CampusConnect, you'll need:

  • At least 5 interested student members
  • A designated student leader or coordinator
  • A brief outline of your proposed club activities and goals
